# ANNEX — UNESCO Hackathon Pitch Video Script

> **Project:** ANNEX — AI-powered Media & Information Literacy platform
> **Runtime:** 3:00 (≈ 430 spoken words) · **Format:** 6 beats with on-screen cues
> **One-liner:** *Learn Before You Believe.*

Every timecode includes the narration and the visuals that should be on screen.
Keep cuts fast, captions on, and let the product demos carry the story.

---

## Beat 1 — The problem (0:00 – 0:20)

**Narration:**

> Somewhere right now, a message is being shared that isn't true. It spreads
> faster than any correction ever will. By the time the fact-checkers respond,
> millions of people have already seen it — and too many have believed it.
>
> The problem isn't that people don't care about the truth. The problem is that
> they don't have a fast, trustworthy way to check what they're about to share.

**On screen:**

- Quick montage: a phone notification, a forward in a group chat, a viral
  headline, a retweet — accelerating, slightly distorted as it "spreads."
- Overlay text: *"Misinformation moves faster than correction."*

---

## Beat 2 — The idea (0:20 – 0:45)

**Narration:**

> We built ANNEX to fix that. ANNEX is an open-source, AI-powered Media and
> Information Literacy platform that helps people verify news articles, claims,
> images, and sources — **before** they share them.
>
> Our mission is simple: *Learn Before You Believe.* ANNEX doesn't just tell you
> what to think. It teaches you how to check — and it shows its work on every
> single verdict.

**On screen:**

- ANNEX logo animates in with the tagline "Learn Before You Believe."
- Product surfaces flash: phone app, web app, browser extension.
- Three icons appear as they're named: a quote (claims), a picture (images),
  a newspaper (sources).

---

## Beat 3 — The demo (0:45 – 1:45)

**Narration:**

> Here's what that looks like in practice. You're reading an article and a claim
> looks suspicious. With the ANNEX browser extension, you don't leave the page —
> you select it, right-click, and verify.
>
> ANNEX decomposes the text into individual claims, scores each one's
> verifiability, and pulls up supporting or contradicting evidence — with links
> you can check yourself.
>
> Now a photo gets forwarded to you, supposedly from a breaking event. ANNEX runs
> OCR to read the embedded text and image forensics to detect tampering signals —
> catching out-of-context and manipulated images that would fool a casual reader.
>
> And what about the source itself? ANNEX scores publishers and domains across
> multiple trust signals, with a transparent, explainable credibility score —
> never a black box.
>
> Then the most important part: instead of just leaving you with a verdict, ANNEX
> teaches. A built-in media-literacy curriculum — interactive lessons on spotting
> misinformation, understanding credibility scores, and verifying images — is
> available on web and mobile, in every supported language.

**On screen (screen-recorded demos, split quickly):**

1. **Extension:** select text on a news page → context menu → "Verify with
   ANNEX" → score breakdown appears in the popup. *(0:45 – 1:00)*
2. **Image analysis:** drop an image → OCR text + forensics risk signals shown.
   *(1:00 – 1:12)*
3. **Source scoring:** search a publisher → credibility score with trust signals
   and rationale. *(1:12 – 1:22)*
4. **Lessons:** curriculum list → open a lesson → mark complete; progress bar
   fills. *(1:22 – 1:45)*

---

## Beat 4 — Why it's different (1:45 – 2:20)

**Narration:**

> What makes ANNEX different? Three things.
>
> **First, transparency.** Every score explains itself. Users see the evidence,
> the signals, and the reasoning — so they learn how to judge, not just what to
> accept.
>
> **Second, it meets people where they are.** Mobile, web, and a browser
> extension — verification lives inside the reading experience, not in a
> separate fact-checking portal.
>
> **Third, it's multilingual by design.** Seven languages are already seeded —
> English, Portuguese, Spanish, French, German, Arabic, and Japanese — and new
> languages are added server-side, with no app rebuild. Media literacy shouldn't
> be gated by language.
>
> And it's privacy-preserving and fully open source under Apache 2.0 — built for
> communities and institutions to run and trust.

**On screen:**

- Split screen: score explanation panel expands showing evidence links.
- Map or globe with language flags lighting up as each language is named.
- Open-source badges: Apache 2.0, GitHub.

---

## Beat 5 — Impact & sustainability (2:20 – 2:45)

**Narration:**

> ANNEX is not a prototype — it's a working, production-grade platform. A
> hardened FastAPI backend, AI analysis with OCR and image forensics, secure
> authentication, and a full CI/CD pipeline deploying to the cloud on every
> release.
>
> Our roadmap is to take it further: educator tools to assign lessons and track
> class progress, community-contributed translations, and a public source
> credibility registry that grows more accurate the more it's used.
>
> Because the real cure for misinformation isn't a single fact-check — it's a
> generation of people who know how to check for themselves.

**On screen:**

- CI pipeline animation: commits → tests → deploy → live app.
- Roadmap cards: "Educator tools", "Community translations", "Source registry".
- Final shot: students/readers on phones and laptops.

**Narration:**

> UNESCO's mission — empowering people with the skills to access, analyze, and

>
> We're asking for your support to scale ANNEX into the communities that need it

>
> Learn before you believe. That's ANNEX.

**On screen:**

## Production notes (for the team)

- **Timing:** Rehearse to land at ~3:00; ~430 words at a measured 140 wpm. If you
  run long, trim Beat 4 (keep the three differentiators) before touching the
  demo beat — the demo is the heart of the video.
- **Captions:** Always on; add them in the editor so they're readable on a phone
  with no sound.
- **Demo recording:** Record at 1080p, clean browser UI (hide bookmarks),
  real-looking sample content, and cursor movements that are slow and deliberate.
- **Music/voice:** Light, forward-moving music; cut it to silence under narration
  so scores and announcements land.
- **End card:** Hold the closing card ≥ 4 seconds with the repository URL
  visible — judges will screenshot it.
